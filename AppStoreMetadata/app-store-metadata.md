## Description

PlayItRight is a complete video player and media library for Apple TV 4K.

PLAY EVERYTHING
MKV, MP4, ISO, BDMV, WEBM, and more. Apple hardware acceleration is used whenever the Apple TV supports the format, with bundled software decoding only for the formats it cannot play natively — so Dolby Vision, HDR10, HDR10+, HLG, Dolby Atmos, and DTS content plays on any display.

A BEAUTIFUL LIBRARY
Automatic metadata, artwork, cast, ratings, and trailers from any source, organized the way you think. The library is backed by a local media database keyed on TMDB/TVDB ids with full cast and crew records.

EVERY SOURCE
Plex, Jellyfin, Emby, SMB, NFS, WebDAV, FTP/SFTP, UPnP, cloud drives, and local files.

SYNCED AND READY
Libraries, watch state, and in-progress resume across your devices, plus AirPlay and Google Cast.

HONEST ABOUT QUALITY
Every title shows what is actually happening: direct play, remux, hardware transcode, or software transcode. No guessed badges and no silent quality drops.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No telemetry, no advertising SDKs, and no account required. Source credentials stay in the Keychain on your Apple TV. App Privacy: Data Not Collected.

PlayItRight Pro unlocks every video codec, lossless audio, unlimited sources, cloud drives, iCloud sync, trailers, and casting. HDR10, Dolby Vision, and Dolby Atmos playback stay free.
